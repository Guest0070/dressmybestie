# Swap placeholders, tame bloom, ship real thumbnails

The most urgent item for Style My Bestie is outside the code. Since **19 October 2026, a Roblox game with only the default thumbnail gets no Home recommendation impressions at all**, so Divyansh needs to upload 2–5 custom 16:9 thumbnails and a 512×512 icon before launch ([DevForum staff](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/homepage-recommendation-eligibility-now-requires-a-custom-thumbnail/4945086)). On the in-game side, the architecture is already right: classic Sound objects routed through Music and SFX SoundGroups, effects built on the client, a flashing toggle, and imported lights created with `Shadows = false`. The work left is mostly tuning. First, replace the `rbxasset://` placeholder sounds and the `0` music slots with free Creator Store or self-uploaded audio IDs. Second, fix the washed-out look where it starts, in Bloom Threshold and a probable Retro ColorGradingEffect, instead of switching all post-processing off in the booths. Third, cap flashes at about 3 per second and respect `GuiService.ReducedMotionEnabled`. Fourth, keep shadow-casting lights to a handful. All of this costs ₹0. One real cost belongs on the launch checklist: to let under-16 players see the game, Roblox's 2026 rules require verification, 2FA, and either two months of Premium or a refundable 1,000 Robux fee ([Roblox Docs: Publish](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places)). The final table separates what Divyansh must click in Studio or the Creator Dashboard from what can be changed in code.

## Free audio exists in two safe forms, and Config.Sounds only needs numbers

Since March 2022, Roblox audio has been private by default. Random IDs copied from "music code" lists mostly fail, and the only sign is an error in the Output window ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/1701697)). If an asset lacks permission, it "cannot load in Studio, and a clickable error message displays in the Output window" ([Asset privacy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/projects/assets/privacy.md)). That leaves two sources that are safe and cost nothing. The first is the Creator Store audio tab, which holds **more than 100,000 free-to-use professional SFX and music tracks** from Roblox and its partners ([Audio assets](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/audio/assets.md)). Roblox has licensed the APM catalog so that sync, master, mechanical and performance rights are covered, with a cap of 250 licensed tracks per game ([Roblox Support](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/articles/360000927163)). About 180,000 DistroKid tracks were added in 2024 ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/roblox-strikes-deal-with-distrokid-but-indie-artists-wont-be-paid-any-money-from-it1)). The second source is audio Divyansh uploads himself. Uploading is free, and the limit is **2,000 imports per 30 days if ID-verified or 100 if not**. Files must be mp3, ogg, wav or flac, under 20 MB, under 7 minutes, and at 48 kHz or less ([Audio assets](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/audio/assets.md)). Audio he owns always plays in his own games. If the game later moves to a group, though, it needs explicit permission grants, and those grants can't be revoked ([Asset privacy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/projects/assets/privacy.md)).

For self-uploads, good free sources are Kenney packs (CC0), Pixabay (commercial use allowed, attribution optional, no standalone resale) ([Pixabay Terms](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/)) and the yearly free Sonniss GDC bundles (royalty-free, no attribution) ([rekkerd.org](https://rekkerd.org/sonniss-releases-gdc-2026-game-audio-bundle/)). On Freesound and OpenGameArt, check the licence of each file. Avoid CC-BY-NC, because the game sells products, and avoid CC-BY-SA. Studio's own steps are short. Open Toolbox > Creator Store > choose Audio in the category dropdown, search, then right-click a result and choose **Copy Asset ID**. A left-click inserts a Sound into the Explorer instead ([Audio assets](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/audio/assets.md)). The copied number goes into `Config.Sounds.<Slot>` in `src/shared/Config.luau`, and the code wraps it as `rbxassetid://`.

Keep classic Sound and SoundGroups. The newer Audio API (`AudioPlayer` → `Wire` → `AudioFader` → `AudioDeviceOutput`) is required for true sidechain ducking through `AudioCompressor` ([Roblox Docs: Audio](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/audio.md)). However, SoundGroups don't apply to it, so moving over would mean rewiring `Audio.luau`, `Kit` and `MinigameFx`, and the Settings mutes along with them. The gain doesn't justify that now. Ducking can be faked by tweening `Music.Volume` down 40–60% for 1–2 s during the Podium stinger.

The table below assigns each slot a sound. Lengths follow common UI-sound rules of thumb: taps of 30–150 ms, pitch randomised by about 0.95–1.05 so repeats don't grate, a rising pitch for confirm and a falling pitch for cancel, and negative sounds that are never harsh ([Bugnet](https://bugnet.io/blog/how-to-make-ui-sounds-satisfying); [Audiokinetic](https://www.audiokinetic.com/en/approaching-ui-audio-ui-design-perspective-2)). Dress to Impress reviewers describe the same genre formula: a looping theme soundtrack, satisfying item-select sounds and crowd reactions on the runway ([Varsity](https://varsity.co.uk/fashion/28397)).

| Slot | Current | Replace with | Length / volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Click | clickfast.wav | soft tick or pop, no hover sound | 30–100 ms, 0.3–0.4, random pitch |
| PhaseChange | switch.wav | short riser or chime (same family for every phase) | 0.5–1.5 s, medium |
| Heart (vote cast) | snap.mp3 | warm rising chime, identical for every score | 150–300 ms, low-medium |
| Reaction | snap.mp3 | tiny pop; cap at about 3 overlapping | <150 ms, low |
| Podium | victory.wav | drum roll into cheer; top 3 only, duck music | 2–4 s, high |
| LobbyMoment | Launching rocket.wav | short fanfare | 1–2 s, medium |
| Alert | switch.wav (same as PhaseChange) | soft low "bonk", distinct from PhaseChange | 100–200 ms, low |
| RunwayMusic | 0 (built-in beat) | upbeat house or fashion beat from the Creator Store | loop, 0.4–0.5 |
| LobbyMusic | 0 (none) | chill pop or lounge loop | loop, 0.25–0.35 |

Several code changes are worth making. Give Alert its own sound so an error doesn't sound like a phase change. Crossfade music between phases over about 1 s instead of cutting hard. Turn the Music and SFX mute switches into stepped volumes (0/25/50/75/100%) that set `SoundGroup.Volume`. Because SoundGroup volume multiplies each Sound's volume, one fader per bus is enough ([SoundGroup](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/SoundGroup)). Every audio cue already needs a matching visual cue, since many mobile players play muted. Normalise uploaded files to similar loudness in Audacity before uploading them. Keep a simple `assets/audio/LICENSES.md` log of each file's source URL and licence, in case a copyright claim ever comes in. One question is still open: the "Using Licensed Music in Videos" help article couldn't be read, so it is unconfirmed whether YouTubers recording the game with licensed tracks can get Content ID claims.

## Code-built VFX is the right design; flashing rules need tightening

Building effects on the client is the documented best practice. The server validates the moment and fires a small trigger, and each client builds the effect locally. Replicated effects are "slow and laggy" ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/client-sided-vfx/2792835)). This also means each viewer's own flashing setting is honoured, which server-replicated effects can't do. Players who join late don't receive past `FireAllClients` calls, so any effect that is still running, such as an aura, has to be replayed for them ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/2323228)).

Use the toolkit as Roblox documents it. Call `ParticleEmitter:Emit(n)` for bursts (default 16) instead of high constant Rates. Changing emitter properties often "can have a dramatic impact on performance", so configure emitters once and fire `Emit` ([Improve performance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/improve)). Use `LockedToPart` so outfit sparkles move with a walking avatar. Use `TimeScale = 0` for a cheap freeze-frame. Flipbooks accept the `Grid2x2/4x4/8x8/Custom` layouts and play at up to 30 fps ([ParticleEmitter](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ParticleEmitter)). The older `TwoByTwo`-style enum names in tutorials no longer work ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/particles’-flipbook-release/2029388)). Low-memory phones switch flipbooks off automatically, so every effect must still look acceptable without its animation ([Particle emitters](https://create.roblox.com/docs/effects/particle-emitters)). Call `Trail:Clear()` before teleporting a model to the runway start so it doesn't drag a streak behind it ([Trail](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Trail)). The Highlight cap is now **255 at once, not the often-quoted 31**, and a disabled Highlight still takes a slot. Highlighting the walker and the top three is fine as long as each Highlight is destroyed after use ([Highlight](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Highlight)).

The runway reads best as a set of fakes, because Roblox has no volumetric light. A SpotLight lights the walker, and a Beam cone from the ceiling shows the shaft: small `Width0`, large `Width1`, Transparency fading from 0.6 to 1, `LightEmission` 1 and a slow `TextureSpeed` ([Beam](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Beam)). For paparazzi flashes, pulse a small PointLight and emit one star particle at random audience positions. Podium confetti is 2–3 emitters with Rate 0 and one or two `Emit(40–60)` calls. Nothing should mark last place. For textures, draw white shapes (heart, star, sparkle, confetti strip, soft glow) at 128² or 256² and tint them through `Color`. A 1024² texture uses four times the memory of a 512², and most minor images should be under 256² ([Improve performance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/improve)). Kenney's Particle Pack is CC0 and has 80 sprites ([OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/particle-pack-80-sprites)). Treat Creator Store VFX models only as donors of texture IDs: insert one into an empty baseplate, copy the IDs, then delete it. Backdoors usually come in through scripts hidden inside free models ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/removing-backdoors-101/545574)).

The accessibility gap is the most important VFX fix. Roblox's accessibility guidelines say **nothing** about flashing or photosensitivity, but they do expose `GuiService.ReducedMotionEnabled` as a player preference to respect ([Roblox Accessibility](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/accessibility)). The usable standard is WCAG 2.3.1: **no more than three flashes in any one-second period**, with saturated red flashes treated as the most dangerous ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/three-flashes-or-below-threshold.html)). A runway shot fills most of a phone screen, so the small-area exemption rarely applies. Two settings matter here. `PlayerData`'s template currently defaults `flashes = true`, and nothing in the code reads `ReducedMotionEnabled`. The code fix has three parts. Add a global flash limiter in `MinigameFx` that counts every flash source together and allows no more than about 2–3 per second even when flashing is on. Default flashing to off, and turn off sweeping beams and camera shake, when `ReducedMotionEnabled` is true. Show a one-time first-join notice that points to Settings. Roblox doesn't require the notice. Even so, a 2025–2026 report linked a Roblox game to an alleged seizure death (unconfirmed), and the developers then added a warning ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78238-roblox-game-allegedly-linked-to-player-s-epilepsy-death-developers-criticised-for-negligence)).

## The washed-out booths point to Bloom Threshold and Retro grading, not post-processing itself

`Stage.luau` turns off every Lighting post-effect during styling, and `Map.luau` removed the booth roof, both because rooms "washed out". Both are workarounds. The documented behaviour suggests three causes. The first is `BloomEffect.Threshold`. At 1 only pure white blooms, and at 0 every colour blooms ([BloomEffect](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/BloomEffect)). Bloom and Neon glow are the same post-effect, so a low Threshold turns ordinary lit mid-tones milky ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/neon-material-too-bright/2497344)). The second is the **ColorGradingEffect**. It has only two presets, and Retro makes colours "less saturated" with "less contrast". Only the most recently parented instance in Lighting is applied ([ColorGradingEffect](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ColorGradingEffect)). Places that were ever on Compatibility lighting were migrated automatically to **Voxel + a Retro ColorGradingEffect** when opened in Studio ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/compatibility-lighting-becomes-retro-tone-mapping-sunset-migration/3128560)). The third is dark interiors compensated with exposure. A dusk ClockTime gives a closed room almost no sky light. Raising ExposureCompensation or bloom to make up for it blows out the neon and greys the darks. Raising **Ambient** a little does the job without that side effect. Ambient is the hue "applied to areas that are occluded from the sky, such as indoor areas" ([Lighting](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Lighting)).

Lighting mode is a Studio job for Divyansh. `Lighting.Technology` is deprecated and can't be scripted. It has been replaced by `LightingStyle` (Realistic or Soft) and `PrioritizeLightingQuality`, and old Future maps to Realistic with Prioritize on ([Technology enum](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/enums/Technology); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-there-be-unified-light-unified-lighting-is-fully-live/3401512)). Roblox says scripts can't set the new properties either, although the API dump marks them as scriptable. Set them in the Properties window and File > Save to Roblox. Don't rely on code for this. For a neon, glossy venue, the right setting is Realistic with Prioritize on. Smooth marble and glass reflect only when `EnvironmentSpecularScale` is above 0, and its default is 0 ([Lighting](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Lighting)). Glass refraction **does not work on mobile**, so glass capsules have to look right as a single tinted transparent layer ([Materials](https://create.roblox.com/docs/parts/materials)).

The values below are starting points worked out from the documented meaning of each property. Roblox publishes no presets, so tune them by eye. Studio may also hide some effects unless Studio Settings > Rendering > Editor Quality Level is at maximum ([Post-processing](https://create.roblox.com/docs/environment/post-processing-effects)).

| Setting (in Lighting) | Start at | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LightingStyle / PrioritizeLightingQuality | Realistic / true | per-pixel local lights, specular highlights |
| Ambient | about RGB(50,40,60) | lifts indoor darks without exposure |
| ExposureCompensation | 0 to −0.3 | avoid blowing out neon |
| EnvironmentSpecularScale / DiffuseScale | 0.8 / 0.3 | reflections on marble, glass and metal |
| Bloom Intensity / Size / Threshold | 0.5 / 24 / 1.0 | only Neon and strong lights glow |
| ColorGradingEffect | Default, or delete it | Retro washes colour out |
| ColorCorrection Brightness / Contrast / Saturation | 0 / 0.1 / 0.1 | gentle lift; check that TintColor is white |

In code, replace the switch-everything-off approach in `Stage.luau` with **per-phase presets on `Workspace.CurrentCamera`**. Post-effects parented to the Camera show only for that player ([Post-processing](https://create.roblox.com/docs/environment/post-processing-effects)). A styling-booth preset tweens Lighting bloom Intensity to 0, applies a neutral ColorCorrection and adds a white light over the mannequin. A runway preset tweens Contrast and Saturation up to about 0.2 and bloom up to about 0.8, keeping Threshold at 0.95 or above. Two caveats apply. Bloom instances combine, so stacking a Camera bloom on the Lighting bloom brightens rather than replaces. ColorGrading works only in Lighting.

Performance comes from light shadows. Roblox singles out "a high number and density of light objects that cast shadows" as a cost. Its advice is to turn off `Light.Shadows` where it isn't needed, set `CastShadow = false` on small parts, limit Range, and switch lights off room by room indoors. Shadows **turn off entirely below quality level 4**, so the venue has to look good without them ([Improve performance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/improve)). `BlendImport.luau` already creates every imported light with `Shadows = false` and clamps Range to 6–60 studs. The next code steps are three. Turn shadows on for only 2–4 runway hero SpotLights. Set `CastShadow = false` on LED strips, Neon, glass and small decor meshes. Disable lights in zones the player can't see from the client. Game scripts can't read the real graphics level, because `SavedQualityLevel` returns Automatic for most players ([UserGameSettings](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/UserGameSettings.md)). A "Low graphics" toggle in Settings is therefore the reliable lever. Profile on a real phone with the MicroProfiler scopes `computeLightingPerform` and `ShadowMapSystem`. Roblox publishes no light-count budget, so measuring is the only way to know.

## Discovery now runs on 16:9 thumbnails, so make 3–5 of them honest

The marketing art has changed in two ways this year. From March 2026, Search shows **16:9 thumbnails instead of 1:1 icons** ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/search-results-will-now-show-169-thumbnails/4525356)). From October 2026, Home recommendations require a custom thumbnail ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/homepage-recommendation-eligibility-now-requires-a-custom-thumbnail/4945086)). The specs are simple. The icon is square, at least 512×512, and checked at about 150 px. Thumbnails are 16:9, ideally 1920×1080 and under 3 MB. The detail page holds up to 10 media items. Video uploads are free, three a month, and rejected uploads still count against that quota ([Icons](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-icons); [Thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)). Upload them at Creator Dashboard > game > Configurations > Places > start place > Icon / Thumbnails, which has Home Page and Experience Detail Page tabs.

The ranking signals reward honest art. Since June 2026 the Recommended-for-You algorithm weighs **play-through rate and first-play bounce rate (under 60 s and 61–180 s)** ([Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)). A thumbnail that promises a dazzling runway and then delivers a minute in the lobby gets clicks and also gets penalised. Make one thumbnail per phase (secret styling at the booth, the runway walk, the star-rating reveal) with real in-game UI, and keep anything important away from the bottom edge, where metadata covers it. For the icon, show two expressive avatars (stylist and bestie), use a saturated pink and pastel palette, add one strong prop, and use three words of text or fewer. Show it to five people who don't know the game and ask "what is this?" ([creatorxp.gg](https://creatorxp.gg/guides/roblox-game-icon-mistakes)). Avoid any Robux or "Free UGC" claims and avoid imitating Dress to Impress. Both can reduce exposure for "giveaway" or "non-unique" metadata ([Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)).

The video rules are the strictest. It must be **real gameplay** (freecam angles, light brightness and contrast edits, and a logo are fine). It must contain no voice-over and no music with lyrics, music must come only from Roblox's audio catalog, and it can't use promotional text or visuals "enhanced beyond what players see" ([Thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)). The free workflow uses three tools. Use Studio's freecam (Left Shift+P in a solo playtest) for screenshots and video ([Free camera](https://create.roblox.com/docs/workspace/camera/free-camera)). Export posed avatars from Studio as .fbx or .gltf, import them into the existing Blender venue scene and render at 1920×1080 ([Blender](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/blender); [avatar export](https://create.roblox.com/docs/avatar/character-bodies/export)). Add text in Photopea or GIMP. Because of the "accurately reflect" rule, Blender renders should match the in-game look. Turn on Thumbnail Personalization with 2–5 active thumbnails. It is a multi-armed bandit that early adopters saw lift qualified play-through rate by an average of 8.5% ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/live-now-personalize-your-thumbnails-to-attract-more-users/3257233)). Roblox has no icon A/B test, so change the icon one variant at a time, about a week each, and log the dates.

## Conclusion

Most of the work is configuration, not new systems. The audio and VFX code is already built correctly: client-side, routed through SoundGroups, with a flashing toggle. The quality gap comes from placeholder IDs and a few unsafe defaults. The booth wash-out shows how a workaround can hide the real cause. Disabling all post-processing fixed the symptom, but the likely causes are a low Bloom Threshold or a migrated Retro grading effect sitting in Lighting. Fixing them in Studio and adding per-phase Camera presets would bring back a glamorous runway look without bleaching outfits. Divyansh should check Lighting's children in Explorer before anyone writes more code around this.

Of the four areas, marketing graphics now has the most leverage. With Home requiring custom thumbnails and Search showing 16:9 art, a game with good gameplay and default thumbnails gets almost no traffic. Because ranking punishes early bounces, the best thumbnails show what the first three minutes actually deliver. Three questions remain open, and only testing will settle them: how many of the ~120 lights a low-end phone handles under Realistic lighting, whether a script can really set `LightingStyle`, and how YouTube treats licensed Roblox music in creators' videos.
